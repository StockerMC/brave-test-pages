$popup filter test

  1. Add as a custom filter (Brave: brave://settings/shields/filters, with brave://flags/#brave-adblock-popup-blocking on).
  2. For each row, click reset, then the button or link. uBO lets through the last link you clicked, so reset keeps one case from affecting the next.
  3. The result fills in after a few seconds. Where the page can't see the window, click what happened. Blocked means a tab or window opened and closed right away.

Expected is for Brave with the flag on. With Shields down or the flag off, everything opens.
