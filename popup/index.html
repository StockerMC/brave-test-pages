$popup filter test

Setup:

  1. Enable brave://flags/#brave-adblock-popup-blocking and relaunch.
  2. Add this line under Custom filters in brave://settings/shields/filters and save: ||example.org^$popup
  3. Keep Shields up on this page.

Run each test once, top to bottom. Tests 1 to 3 fill in their own result. Tests 4 to 6 need you to look at what the tab does.

#TestExpectedResult
1 A tab opens and closes right away. example.org never loads.
2 example.com opens in a new tab and stays open.
3 A blank tab opens and closes within a second.
4 A tab opens and closes right away. check the tab
5 A tab opens and closes right away. check the tab
6 Link to example.org Click it, or focus it and press Enter. example.org opens and stays open, because you clicked the link yourself. check the tab
